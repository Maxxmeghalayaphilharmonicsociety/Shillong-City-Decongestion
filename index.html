Preamble

This representation places on record institutional contradictions and statutory violations regarding the ₹151-Crore Shillong Peak Ropeway Project. While pitched as a decongestion solution, the project imposes concrete construction within sensitive forest and water catchment areas.

Question 1: Decongestion & New Shillong Township (NST) Failure

Why should the government proceed with this ropeway when its primary justification (decongestion) mirrors the failed promises of NST?

Question 2: Hydrology & EIA Exemption

How can the project claim a 0.2% footprint while building heavy RCC terminal structures inside Upper Shillong’s vital water catchments and spring recharge zones?

Question 3: Military Security & Cantonment Double Standards

Why was the Rilbong-Barik flyover scrapped over Army security concerns while 13 elevated commercial gondola cabins overlook the Eastern Air Command radar station?
