Forensic Audit & Fact-Check Report: Shillong Peak Ropeway

Published: November 14, 2026 • Verified Public-Domain Records[cite: 5]

1. Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Appraisal

Official statements claiming EIA was never required are contradicted by MoEF&CC PARIVESH records showing appraisal at the 66th and 73rd EAC Meetings under Schedule Item 7(g) of the EIA Notification, 2006[cite: 5].

2. Co-Financing & Non-Duplication Conflict

The project combines an ₹80.89 Cr NDB sovereign loan with a ₹70.11 Cr PM-DevINE grant, implicating PM-DevINE Section 2.2 and 7.1.5(ii) restrictions against funding projects already covered under other schemes[cite: 5].

3. Water Extraction & Hydrological Risk

Table 14 plans for 211.5 KLD of water extraction at the Lum Shyllong recharge zone despite 80% of consulted residents warning of spring depletion and water security risks[cite: 5].
